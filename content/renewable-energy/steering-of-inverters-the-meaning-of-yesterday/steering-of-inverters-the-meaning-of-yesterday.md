# Audience

This article is written for those, who struggle to understand the rationale behind certain Inverter Registers.

# The Meaning of &laquo; Yesterday &raquo;

Most Inverters have a Register called __Energy Today__ and a Register called __Energy Yesterday__.

If you wonder why this article is for you.

## &laquo; Today &raquo;

&laquo; Today &raquo; has one interesing quality. It is not complete until it is over and when it is over it is not anymore &laquo; Today &raquo;, but becomes &laquo; Yesterday &raquo;.

## &laquo; Yesterday &raquo;

&laquo; Yesterday &raquo; has the interesting quality. It holds the value of &laquo; Today &raquo; the moment &laquo; Today &raquo; becomes complete.

# Conclusion

Therefore &laquo; Yesterday &raquo; is not a random quirk by the people who wrote the software of the Inverter.

But the holder of &laquo; Today &raquo;

# How is Energy Yesterday Useful?

Energy Yesterday is useful, because it allows the reading of the Energy Produced by the Inverter without a dedicated Power Meter.

Power Meters are not inexpensive and the ability to read the Energy Produced by the Inverter _from_ the Inverter reduces the cost of the Installation.
